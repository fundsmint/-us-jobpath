JOB SAFETY

How to recognise fake US job offers

Job seekers should verify employers before sharing personal information, paying fees, or making travel plans. Scammers may copy real company names and logos.

Warning signs

  • A recruiter promises guaranteed employment or a guaranteed visa.
  • You are asked to pay for an offer letter, interview, or guaranteed placement.
  • The email address or website does not match the real employer's official domain.
  • The job offers unusually high pay for very little work and no clear requirements.
  • You are pressured to act immediately or send sensitive documents before the employer is verified.
  • The person sends a cheque and asks you to return part of the money or buy equipment from a specific vendor.

How to verify a vacancy

Find the employer's official website independently and locate its careers page. Check whether the vacancy appears there, and contact the organisation through contact details listed on that official site. Do not rely only on a link sent by a stranger.

Protect your information

Do not send bank passwords, one-time passcodes, or unnecessary identity information to an unverified person. If you suspect fraud, stop communicating and report it to the relevant platform or authorities.
