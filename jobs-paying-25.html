SALARY GUIDE

How to research US jobs that pay $25 an hour or more

A practical starting guide · Always verify current vacancy details

A job paying $25 an hour can be an appealing target, but wages vary by state, city, employer, experience, shift, and qualifications. Treat salary examples as a starting point for research, not a promise of income.

1. Search by job title and pay

Use reputable job boards to discover vacancies, then open the employer's official careers page to confirm the position is real and still open. Search for specific titles such as IT support specialist, skilled-trade apprentice, customer support specialist, or commercial driver—but check the requirements for each role.

2. Check the full requirements

Read the qualifications, licensing requirements, work schedule, location, and experience level. A job title alone does not tell you whether you qualify. Some roles require a licence, certification, background check, or previous experience.

3. Understand the annual equivalent

At 40 paid hours per week for 52 weeks, $25 per hour equals about $52,000 per year before taxes. Actual earnings may be lower if hours vary or time off is unpaid. Taxes, benefits, overtime rules, and cost of living affect the value of an offer.

4. Check work eligibility

If you live outside the United States, check whether the employer accepts international applicants and whether you already need US work authorization. A US-based company or remote label does not automatically make a role available worldwide.

5. Keep your search safe

Use official employer websites, be careful with unsolicited offers, and do not pay someone who promises a guaranteed job. Confirm any claimed salary and sponsorship directly with the employer.
