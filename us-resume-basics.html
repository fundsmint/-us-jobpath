APPLICATION TIPS

Preparing a résumé for US job applications

A résumé gives an employer a quick view of your relevant skills, experience, education, and achievements. Tailor it to each role and keep every claim truthful.

Common sections

  • Contact details: name, professional email, and appropriate phone contact.
  • Summary: a short introduction focused on relevant strengths.
  • Skills: skills requested in the vacancy that you genuinely have.
  • Experience: roles, dates, responsibilities, and measurable results where possible.
  • Education and certifications: relevant qualifications and training.

Keep it clear

Use consistent headings, readable formatting, and concise bullet points. Where appropriate, describe accomplishments with concrete results. Avoid spelling errors and do not claim qualifications you do not have.

Personal information

For many US applications, a photo, date of birth, marital status, or other personal details are not necessary on a résumé. Follow the employer's instructions and avoid including sensitive information that is not needed.

Tailor before submitting

Compare your résumé with the job requirements and move the most relevant experience higher. Save a copy for each application and check the employer's official submission instructions.
