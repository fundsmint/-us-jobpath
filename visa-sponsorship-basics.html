VISA BASICS

How to research US jobs with visa sponsorship

Some US employers sponsor eligible workers for certain immigration or temporary-work categories. Sponsorship depends on the job, the employer, the visa category, and the applicant’s circumstances. It is not available for every job.

Start with official information

Learn about work visa categories through the US government’s official immigration and employment resources. Requirements can change, so avoid relying only on social media posts or an agent's promises.

Verify the employer's claim

Check the job on the employer's official careers site. Look for clear statements about sponsorship or ask the employer's authorised recruiting team. Do not label a vacancy “visa-sponsored” just because the company has sponsored someone in the past.

Be alert to scams

Be cautious if someone demands money in exchange for a guaranteed job, visa approval, or interview. Verify email addresses and company details independently. Do not send identity documents or sensitive information to an unverified contact.

Prepare a realistic application

Match your experience and qualifications to the advertised role. Keep your résumé accurate and gather any relevant certificates. If you need advice about your individual immigration situation, consult a qualified immigration attorney or authorised professional.

This page is general information, not legal or immigration advice.
