Where to look for legitimate US vacancies

Job boards can help you discover opportunities, but the employer's official listing is the best place to confirm job details before applying.

Try more than one route

  • Employer career pages: search the company name and navigate to its official website yourself.
  • Government-supported resources: use official public employment resources for guidance and job-search tools.
  • Professional networks: research the employer and recruiter rather than trusting a profile alone.
  • Industry-specific boards: check the board's reputation and confirm each vacancy with the employer.

Use focused search terms

Search for the role, location, required experience, pay range, and—if relevant—whether international applicants are considered. Avoid assuming that a listing offers sponsorship unless the employer confirms it.

Keep track of applications

Record the job title, employer, official application link, date applied, contact details, and next step. This makes it easier to spot duplicates and follow up professionally.

Before submitting

Check that the listing is still open, read the qualifications, and never pay for a guaranteed job. Be cautious about requests for financial information before a legitimate hiring process reaches the appropriate stage.
